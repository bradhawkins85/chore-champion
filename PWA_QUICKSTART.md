# ChoreQuest PWA - Quick Start

## What is a PWA?

ChoreQuest is a Progressive Web App (PWA) - a modern web application that works like a native mobile app. You can install it on your device's home screen and use it just like any other app!

## Why Install ChoreQuest as an App?

✅ **Works Offline** - Complete chores even without internet  
✅ **Faster** - Instant loading after first install  
✅ **Convenient** - One tap from your home screen  
✅ **Full Screen** - No browser bars, more screen space  
✅ **Private** - All data stays on your device  

## How to Install

### On iPad/iPhone (Recommended for Kids)

1. Open ChoreQuest in **Safari** (must be Safari!)
2. Tap the **Share** button at the bottom (square with arrow)
3. Scroll down and tap **"Add to Home Screen"**
4. Tap **"Add"** in the top right
5. Find the ChoreQuest icon on your home screen!

### On Android Tablet/Phone

1. Open ChoreQuest in Chrome
2. Tap the **menu** (three dots) in the top right
3. Tap **"Install app"** or **"Add to Home screen"**
4. Tap **"Install"**
5. Find ChoreQuest in your app drawer!

### On Computer

1. Open ChoreQuest in Chrome, Edge, or Brave
2. Look for the **install icon** (⊕) in the address bar
3. Click it and choose **"Install"**
4. ChoreQuest opens in its own window!

## Tips for Parents

- Install on each child's tablet for easy access
- The app works offline after the first load
- All chore data is saved automatically
- Weather features require internet
- Use Parent Mode PIN to keep settings secure

## Tips for Kids

- Tap the ChoreQuest icon on your home screen
- Complete chores even when offline
- Check your points anytime
- Browse the reward shop
- Track your goal progress

## Need Help?

If the app doesn't install:
- Make sure you're using the right browser (Safari on iOS, Chrome on Android)
- Check that you have a stable internet connection
- Try closing and reopening the browser
- Ask a parent for help!

## Privacy & Data

- All your data stays on your device
- No tracking or ads
- Safe for kids to use
- Parent controls included
